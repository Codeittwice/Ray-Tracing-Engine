\section*{List of Symbols}
\label{section:symbols}

The following symbols are used repeatedly throughout the ray-tracing model and results
discussion. \autoref{symbollist} summarizes the most important optical and geometric
quantities, their units, and their unit abbreviations.

\begin{table}[H]
\rowcolors{2}{gray!10}{white}
\centering
\caption{List of Symbols}
\label{symbollist}
\begin{tabular}[t]{m{0.18\textwidth}m{0.34\textwidth}m{0.24\textwidth}m{0.16\textwidth}}
\toprule
\textbf{Symbol} & \textbf{Meaning} & \textbf{Unit} & \textbf{Unit abbreviation} \\
\midrule
$G_\mathrm{DNI}$ & Direct normal irradiance incident from the sun & Watts per square metre & W\,m$^{-2}$ \\
$\Phi$ & Irradiance or receiver flux & Watts per square metre & W\,m$^{-2}$ \\
$\Phi_\mathrm{peak}$ & Maximum receiver flux over all receiver grid cells & Watts per square metre & W\,m$^{-2}$ \\
$C_\mathrm{peak}$, CR & Peak concentration ratio, $\Phi_\mathrm{peak}/G_\mathrm{DNI}$ & Dimensionless & - \\
$\eta_\mathrm{opt}$ & Optical efficiency, $P_\mathrm{abs}/(G_\mathrm{DNI}A_\mathrm{ap})$ & Dimensionless & - \\
$P$ & Optical power carried by a ray & Watts & W \\
$P_\mathrm{ray}$ & Initial power assigned to one primary ray & Watts & W \\
$P_\mathrm{abs}$ & Total power absorbed by the receiver & Watts & W \\
$A_\mathrm{ap}$ & Aperture area from which primary rays are sampled & Square metres & m$^2$ \\
$N$ & Number of primary rays in a trace & Dimensionless count & - \\
$\mathbf{x}$ & Position along a ray & Metres & m \\
$\mathbf{d}$ & Unit ray direction vector & Dimensionless & - \\
$\mathbf{n}$ & Unit surface normal vector & Dimensionless & - \\
$f$ & Paraboloid or lens focal length & Metres & m \\
$D_{90}$ & Diameter enclosing 90\% of absorbed receiver power & Metres & m \\
$\theta_s$ & Solar half-angle used by the pillbox sun model & Radians & rad \\
$\rho$ & Mirror reflectance & Dimensionless & - \\
$n$ & Refractive index of an optical material & Dimensionless & - \\
$R$ & Fresnel reflectance & Dimensionless & - \\
$T$ & Fresnel transmittance, $1-R$ & Dimensionless & - \\
$\alpha$ & Absorption coefficient in Beer--Lambert attenuation & Per metre & m$^{-1}$ \\
$\lambda$ & Wavelength used for spectral material properties & Nanometres & nm \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{table}
